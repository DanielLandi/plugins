# Lyric craft

Songs for music videos: originals that sound like real records and give the video something to show.

## Structure
[Intro] · [Verse 1] · [Pre-Chorus] · [Chorus] · [Verse 2] · [Pre-Chorus] · [Chorus] · [Bridge] · [Final Chorus] · [Outro]
- **Pre-chorus:** 2–4 short lines that rise (shorter lines, rising images, a question) and end unresolved, so the chorus lands.
- **Chorus:** built on the title, 4–6 singable lines, the title in the first or last line.
- **Bridge:** turns the story (a new angle, a confession, a time jump), different line lengths from the verses.
- **Final chorus:** lifts — a changed line, an added backing line, or [Key Change] / [Build] before it.

## Less repetitive
- Verse 2 brings new images; never re-describe verse 1.
- Every repeat of the chorus changes one line or adds a twist; the last one lifts.
- Vary line lengths; mix exact, slant and internal rhymes; no AABB couplets throughout.
- Banned clichés: fire/desire, heart/apart, neon lights, shadows, echoes, "dancing in the rain", "we're on fire",
  "tonight we fly", "lost in the moment", "break these chains", "a million stars". If a line could be in any song, rewrite it.

## More human (cues the generator follows)
- Performance tags on their own line or at a line start: [Whispered], [Spoken], [Harmonies], [Belted], [Breath],
  [Build], [Breakdown], [Instrumental Break], [Guitar Solo].
- Backing vocals and call-and-response in parentheses: `Keep the engine running (keep it running)`.
- One or two ad-libs where a singer would breathe or shout, not on every line.
- Style prompt cues: "live band, room ambience, natural vocal, subtle imperfections"; exclude "autotune" when the
  genre would not use it.

## Built for the video
- Every section carries at least one concrete image that can be animated (a streetlight blinking on a wet road, a
  kitchen at 2 a.m., a cassette spinning). The storyboard is drawn from these.
- Avoid abstract-only sections; a feeling needs a picture.

## Rules
- Originals only; no lines or titles from existing songs; title checked with WebSearch.
- No names of real private people or private references: `validate` checks the workspace's optional
  `private-names.txt` (one name per line), but judgment comes first.
- Under 3000 characters. Section tags in square brackets, one per line.
