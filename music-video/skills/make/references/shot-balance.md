# The balance of camera work in three finished films

Measured from three of the films' own camera code, evaluated at every frame: The End Of The Show (360 s), I Can Make
Anything (221 s) and The Way I Look At You (187 s). They are the source's own music videos, made in the same way (code-rendered, 3D). Use it as a reference when a
section feels too busy or too static. It's a description of what worked, not a target to hit exactly.

## How it was measured

- **Picture speed:** a 5×3 grid of points through each frame, at the subject's distance, projected into the next
  frame's camera. The median of how far they move on screen, in frame widths per second (fw/s). Turning, travel and
  push-ins all count as the eye sees them.
- **Still:** picture speed under 0.04 fw/s.
- **A move** is a burst of picture speed. It's fast at 0.5–1.5 fw/s at its peak, and a whip at 1.5 or more. It **lands**
  when the picture then stays still for 10 frames or more.
- **Size:** the frame's height at the subject, in subject heights (for a person: CU under 0.6 m, MS under 1.6 m, WS
  under 6 m, and so on).

## The whole films

| | The End Of The Show | I Can Make Anything | The Way I Look At You |
|---|---|---|---|
| Still | 40% | 30% | 39% |
| Typical frame's speed (median) | 0.06 fw/s | 0.11 fw/s | 0.09 fw/s |
| Moves per 10 s | 5.8 | 6.9 | 4.9 |
| Fast moves and whips | 82% | 76% | 66% |
| Moves that land and hold | 35% | 24% | 18% |
| Holds: mean / longest | 0.9 / 7.2 s | 1.4 / 9.3 s | 2.6 / 33 s |
| A fast move crosses the frame in (median) | 5 frames | 4 frames | 4 frames |

What they share:
- **Quiet verses.** The camera sits nearly still (0.03–0.04 fw/s typical) for about half the section, with holds of 3–7 s.
- **Energy saved for a few places:** a whirlwind pre-chorus, a chorus that moves.
- **A still stretch near the end:** a 31 s bridge, a 9 s outro hold.

## Two drafts that were sent back (Everybody's Got A Home)

| | First draft | Second draft | The three films |
|---|---|---|---|
| Still | 18% | 17% | 30–40% |
| Typical frame's speed | 0.19 fw/s | 0.15 fw/s | 0.06–0.11 fw/s |
| Moves per 10 s | 6.2 | 8.0 | 4.9–6.9 |
| Mean move | 0.48 s | 0.38 s | 0.46–0.55 s |
| Moves that land | 8% | 14% | 18–35% |

- **The first never settled.** The camera glided all the time, smooth and slow throughout: no quiet verse, no chorus
  move that landed, no close-ups at all, and 63% of it high or slightly high.
- **The second snapped to everything.** Told the camera was too slow, it nearly tripled the whips (28 → 79) and made
  the moves shorter, and it still almost never held still.

The camera rules for the redo came from comparing against the three films above.
