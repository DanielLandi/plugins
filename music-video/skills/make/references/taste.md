# The user's taste

Record the user's verdicts here as they give them: what they liked, what they rejected, and why. Keep the list in
the workspace (`taste.md` next to `config.yaml`) so it survives plugin updates, one line each, with the date and
the video it came from. Add a line only with the user's OK, and read the list before showing any render.

*Example: 2026-01-12 · night-drive · camera moves stay smooth and continuous; never stop and jump.*
